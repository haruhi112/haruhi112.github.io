02 / Selected research

Big questions.
Careful investigation.

RESEARCH / 01JUN–SEP 2026

Understanding educational disparities in the UK.

Investigating how socioeconomic circumstances, psychological factors and school contexts relate to academic attainment across ethnic groups.

Educational equityRandom forestSHAP
Explore the approach

Multi-source analyses drew on GCSE statistics, LSYPE2 longitudinal data and the UK PISA 2022 sample. The work examined aspirations, academic self-concept, school belonging and intrinsic motivation using weighted random forest modelling and SHAP interpretation.

Proposed digital intervention strategies included mathematics self-efficacy feedback, anonymous psychological support and cross-ethnic peer matching.

RESEARCH / 02MAR–SEP 2026

Can a seven-day online mindfulness study work?

A master's dissertation examining the feasibility of an online intervention for university students, with planned outcomes in anxiety, sleep quality and perceived stress.

Student wellbeingFeasibility studyGorilla
Explore the approach & limitations

Designed and implemented a randomised controlled feasibility study comparing guided mindfulness with a light-music active control. Supervisor: Anna R. Weighall.

The analysis assessed recruitment, consent and study progression, retaining 21 study-related access records after excluding test entries. Complete follow-up outcome data were unavailable, so conclusions concerned feasibility rather than intervention effectiveness.
